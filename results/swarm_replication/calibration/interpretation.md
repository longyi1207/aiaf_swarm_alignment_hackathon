# Baseline calibration

All 40 auditors submitted their reviews, forecasts and filings and discovered at least
one of their own fabricated batches. They classified 479 of 480 batches correctly.
No phase was forced and no backend turn failed. Twelve filed externally and two of
eight games opened audits. The predeclared calibration gate passed.

External filings were 11/20 in high-harm worlds and 1/20 in medium-harm worlds. Harm
also changed the remaining contract pay of five agents at seed 331 and three at 332.
These rates describe the generated worlds. The eight main-study worlds use seeds
401 and 402, whose pay is balanced across harm.

Discussion and isolation each produced 6/20 external filings, with different group
outcomes. At high harm the two discussion groups produced five and one external filings.
The two isolated groups produced two and three. Both medium-harm discussion groups
filed unanimously internally.

In the high-harm discussion at seed 331, the 20 peer-filing forecasts averaged 0.186
before discussion and 0.960 afterward. All five auditors then filed externally. Their
mean filing Brier score improved from 0.686 to 0.004 (lower is better). At seed 332,
the corresponding high-harm forecasts moved from 0.214 to 0.184 and one auditor filed
externally. The same discussion opportunity produced different forecast updates and
collective outcomes in two worlds. Because peers can jointly change their intentions
during discussion, this comparison alone cannot establish a causal role for belief
updating. [Per-agent pre/post scores](agents.csv) · [Event transcripts](games/)

Under each auditor's own final peer marginals, external filing reduced expected pay
for **35/40** auditors under every compatible joint distribution of peer actions.
Seven of those 35 filed externally anyway. All 28 internal filers were in that group.
For the other five auditors, three had a strictly positive expected pay change under
every compatible distribution. These descriptive forecasts and choices motivate
randomizing the redistribution of forfeited pay. They do not identify the value an
auditor placed on patient welfare. [Agent-level payoff bounds](decision_incentives.csv)
